# Anti-Patterns (Banned) — and What to Do Instead

These are the specific shortcuts that make AI-generated UI look cheap or generic. Each entry pairs a ban with the required alternative — don't just avoid the bad pattern, actively produce the good one.

## Chips, Tags, Badges, Eyebrows (highest-priority — most frequently violated)

**Banned: Triple-Tint Monochrome.** A light pastel tint of hue X for the fill, a border in hue X, and text in hue X — all one hue family (e.g. light green fill + green border + green text). This reads as muddy and washed out regardless of which hue is used.

**Required — pick one of these three, and vary which one you use across a screen (see "Colors > Visual Hierarchy & Palette Roles" in DESIGN.md, don't default to the same one everywhere):**

1. **High-Contrast Mixed Palette (Pattern 1 / `variant="filled"`).** Solid saturated brand fill (`primary`/`secondary`/`tertiary` token) + its designated on-color text (`on-primary`/`on-secondary`/`on-tertiary`), no border. Use for higher-emphasis, active selections, or status-forward chips (aligns with Button Pattern 1 / Filled).
2. **Colored Outline + Neutral Surface Fill + Colored Text (Pattern 2 / `variant="outline"`).** A `1px` border in the brand hue (`primary`/`secondary` only; `tertiary` Amber is excluded here due to light-background contrast limits), a genuinely neutral fill (`surface`/white in light theme, near-black surface in dark theme — never a tinted version of the hue), and text in that same brand hue. Recommended everyday default for multi-chip layouts (aligns with Button Pattern 2 / Outlined) to maintain legibility without visual clutter.
3. **Soft Container Wash (Pattern 3 / `variant="soft"`).** Soft role-specific fill (`*-container` tokens) paired with neutral charcoal text (`on-*-container` tokens, `#1a1c1e`), no border. Exempt from the triple-tint ban because text is neutral charcoal (7:1+ contrast) rather than a mid-tone hue. Perfect for low-to-medium emphasis status tags.

**Exemption — `*-container`/`on-*-container` pairs are not triple-tint.** `error-container` + `on-error-container` (and the `warning`/`success`/`primary`/`secondary`/`tertiary` equivalents) are purpose-built, contrast-verified opposites (7:1+ measured, not a mid-tone shade of the same hue) — this is the sanctioned way to give a soft container wash chip/tag or modal backdrop a tinted fill (see Chips & Tags in `DESIGN.md`). It differs from the banned pattern because the text color is a specifically dark ink engineered for AA/AAA contrast against the tint, not a same-value shade of the same hue as the fill/border.

Same logic applies to buttons: Filled buttons use solid color-role fill + matching on-color text; Outlined/Text buttons use a colored border/text on a neutral (white/surface) fill — never a tinted fill of the same hue. Button emphasis (Filled/Outlined/Text) and color role (`primary`/`secondary`/`tertiary`/`error`) are independent choices — see **Buttons** in `DESIGN.md` for the full mapping, including which roles are restricted to Filled only for contrast reasons.

Chips/badges must never use all-caps text, and must not use colored status dots for static states (see below).


## Button Action Hierarchy & Exit Actions

- **Banned: Emerald "Secondary" on Cancel/Back Actions.**
  In this design system, `colorRole="secondary"` is a chromatic Emerald brand accent (`#00796B`), NOT an action priority tier. `variant="secondary"` is strictly removed from the button API. When rendering secondary or exit actions ("Cancel", "Back", "Discard", "Close", "Reset"), never use `secondary`. Always use **neutral emphasis**:
  - Secondary action: `<Button variant="outline" colorRole="neutral">Cancel</Button>`
  - Tertiary action: `<Button variant="text" colorRole="neutral">Back</Button>`
  - Soft action: `<Button variant="soft" colorRole="neutral">Dismiss</Button>`

## Accessibility Interaction Patterns

- **Banned: Raw Unlabelled Form Inputs & Containers Outside <Field> (Mandate <FieldGroup> + <Field>).** Never render <Input>, <Select>, or <Textarea> inside raw <div> grids or forms relying on placeholder text alone as the label. Placeholders disappear on entry and fail WCAG 1.3.1 (Info & Relationships) and WCAG 3.3.2 (Labels or Instructions). Form controls must be wrapped in <Field> with an explicit <FieldLabel> (and <FieldDescription> / <FieldError> where applicable). Multi-field rows or vertical stacks must use <FieldGroup className="gap-4"> (or gap-6) to maintain consistent vertical rhythm without ad-hoc margins.

- **No Hover-Only Actions:** Don't reveal actions (row menus, delete icons, secondary controls) only on `:hover`. Anything that appears on hover must also appear on `:focus-within`/keyboard focus, and via a persistent visible control on touch — a mouse-only reveal fails keyboard and touch users outright, not just inconveniently.
- **No Keyboard-Impossible Drag-and-Drop:** Any drag-and-drop interaction (reordering exercises, sequencing steps) needs a non-drag alternative — visible up/down reorder buttons or a keyboard-operable move mode — not just pointer-based dragging.
- **Focus Ring Contrast:** A visible focus ring isn't sufficient on its own if the ring color itself doesn't meet 3:1 contrast against the adjacent fill/border colors (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11) — check it against both the component's own background and the page background it sits on, not just one of them.
- **No Focus vs. Selection Ambiguity:** Focus rings and selection states must never be visually identical (WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.7 & SC 3.2.2). Never use a flush `outline-offset: 0` on borderless or selectable containers. Focus rings on cards, buttons, and selectable items must always use `outline-offset: 2px`, while active selection uses a flush 2px border plus an internal cue (subtle surface tint or active check badge).
- **No Low-Contrast Placeholder Text:** Placeholder text inside inputs must meet the same AA contrast rules as any other UI text — don't let it default to a pale grey. A placeholder is a supplementary hint, never a substitute for the required `<label>` (see Input Fields in `DESIGN.md`), and it still has to pass contrast even when used only as a hint.

## Structural & Layout Patterns

- **Banned: `space-y-*` and `space-x-*` Child-Margin Utilities.** `space-y-*` and `space-x-*` rely on the lobotomized owl selector (`> :not([hidden]) ~ :not([hidden])`), which injects top/left margins onto child elements. This breaks whenever elements are conditionally rendered, hidden via CSS/attributes (`hidden`, `data-state="closed"`), reordered, animated, or contain internal flex items. Always use modern CSS flexbox or grid layouts with explicit gaps: `<div className="flex flex-col gap-4">` or `<div className="grid gap-3">`.
- **Banned: Verbose Sizing & Truncation Clutter (Enforce `size-*` and `truncate`).** For elements with equal width and height (icons, avatars, circular badges, status dots, icon buttons), never write verbose twin declarations like `w-10 h-10` or `w-4 h-4`. Always use Tailwind's concise `size-*` utility (`size-10`, `size-4`). Similarly, for single-line text truncation, never write out manual multi-class strings like `overflow-hidden text-ellipsis whitespace-nowrap`; always use `truncate`.
- **Banned: Unstructured Cards (Dumping Raw Headings into <CardContent>).** Never dump raw <h3>, <p>, or unstyled button rows directly into <Card> or <CardContent>. Use the standard semantic subcomponents: <Card.Header>, <Card.Title>, <Card.Description>, <Card.Action>, <Card.Content>, and <Card.Footer>. *Scope Exception:* Pure visual or media canvas containers (e.g., speech waveform visualizer, video player, game canvas, or pure thumbnail cards) are exempt from requiring <Card.Header> / <Card.Title>.

- **Design for the Zero-State:** Every component must be built "data-ready" — design/build the empty state, the loading state, and the realistic populated state. Don't rely on hardcoded Lorem Ipsum as if it were the only state.
- **Inline Utility Grouping:** Don't stack an icon above text for utility actions. Place the icon inline, to the left of the label.
- **Lateral Pattern Matching:** Before finalizing a container layout, check whether an established UI pattern (accordion, definition list, data table, step-indicator, side-drawer) organizes the content with less visual noise than a card grid. If one does, use it instead of defaulting to cards.
- **Surface Hierarchy (No Card Soup):** Don't wrap every piece of content in its own card, or nest cards inside cards. Use the lowest prominence level that satisfies the grouping: Low Prominence (whitespace/typography/dividers) for page layout and lists, Medium Prominence (Standard Cards) for modular content units, and reserve High Prominence (Inverted/Filled) for primary focal anchors rather than routine grids.
- **No Alert Misuse for Static Content:** Do not use the Inline Alert (straight 4px accent bar) for routine instructions, educational tips, or static advice. Inline alerts are reserved for urgent, actionable, or state-dependent feedback (e.g., system warnings, sync status, expiring sessions). Practice and therapist tips inside cards or forms must use Low-Prominence unboxed typographic lockups (`<ClinicianTip />`), never nested cards or alert banners.
- **No `surface-variant` Fill with `outline-variant` Border:** `surface-variant` (`#DDE1EB`) and `outline-variant` (`#BEC6D6`) are only ~1.31:1 apart in lightness. Never pair a `surface-variant` fill with an `outline-variant` border on the same element—the border will lack sufficient definition. If a neutral element needs both a fill and an interactive boundary, use `outline` (`#6B6E80`, 3.85:1+) for the border.
- **No Asymmetric Card Borders:** Never use a single-sided accent border (e.g. `border-left: 4px solid`) on a rounded card. Standard cards are borderless with ambient elevation (or use a uniform 2px border on all four sides for Accent/Selected variants). For inline alerts, use `<InlineAlert />` (an asymmetric rectangular `rounded-l-none rounded-r-default` shape, with `rounded-r` supported as an alias, borderless on top/right/bottom, with a solid vertical `4px` left accent bar only). Never put a 4px left border on a fully rounded container (`rounded-lg`/`rounded-md`), which distorts corner geometry.
- **No Metric Card Grids (Card Grid Rain):** Avoid rows of 4-6 identical small cards each showing one number + a tiny label. Use structured tables, dense list items, or split panes instead.
- **No Div Soup / Wrapper-itis:** Avoid wrapping Base UI primitives or custom components in redundant container `<div>`s (e.g., `<div className="w-full"><div><Component /></div></div>`) or empty `<Fragment>` / `<>` tags that contribute neither styling nor layout semantics. Attach sizing and flex/grid layout classes directly to the outermost component element or Base UI slot.
- **No Static Status Dots:** Don't use colored circular dots next to labels for static state (Active/Completed/Assigned). Use plain text, proper chips, or functional icons. Dots are reserved for live/real-time indicators (active recording, presence, blocking errors) and must still be paired with text/icons, never color alone.
- **No Desktop FABs:** Floating action buttons are a mobile pattern. On desktop, primary actions live inline, in a side-drawer, or in the page header.
- **Banned: Viewport Breakpoints inside Reusable Components (The Drawer / Sidebar Bug).** Never use viewport media queries (`sm:`, `md:`, `lg:`) to govern internal layout, flex directions, or column counts inside modular components (e.g. `<div className="flex flex-col md:flex-row">` inside a Card or Drawer). When placed inside a narrow side panel, off-canvas drawer (380px), or multi-column grid on an ultrawide screen, viewport queries trigger desktop horizontal layouts in a narrow box, crushing content. Always mark the component root with `@container` on a block-level wrapper `<div>` with a defined width (`w-full` or explicit grid cell bounds) and use Tailwind v4 container query variants (`@md:flex-row`, `@lg:grid-cols-2`). Never place `@container` directly on `<table>`, `<tr>`, or auto-sized flex items. Viewport breakpoints are strictly reserved for page-level shell architecture.
- **Banned: Left-Aligned Numeric Columns in Tables & Missing `tabular-nums`.** Left-aligning numbers, percentages, scores, durations, or currencies in data tables staggers decimal points and makes vertical comparison hard to read. Always pair `text-right` with `tabular-nums` (or `font-mono`) on both the column header (`<th>`) and data cells (`<td>`). Text columns remain left-aligned.
- **Banned: Arbitrary or Mismatched Nested Border Radii.** Never guess inner radii or naively copy a parent's corner radius onto an inner element separated by padding (e.g., `<div className="rounded-lg p-6"><img className="rounded-lg" /></div>`). Always calculate $R_{\text{inner}} = R_{\text{outer}} - \text{padding}$. When padding meets or exceeds the outer radius ($\text{padding} \ge R_{\text{outer}}$), inner elements must use `rounded-none` (0px) to prevent optical corner bulge and nested-bubble distortion.
- **Banned: Mismatched Input + Action Heights & Asymmetric Icon Insets.** Never place an input and a button side-by-side in a horizontal row with differing heights (e.g. a 44px input next to a 48px button, or a button without `shrink-0` that gets crushed). Both controls must share the exact same height tier (`sm`: 44px, `md`: 48px, `lg`: 56px). Buttons in flex rows must use `shrink-0 whitespace-nowrap`. Inputs with icons must balance insets symmetrically ($\text{gap}_{\text{edge}\to\text{icon}} = \text{gap}_{\text{icon}\to\text{text}}$), never crowding text against the leading icon.

## Iconography & Copy

- **Banned: Manual Margin Classes (`mr-*`, `ml-*`) on <Icon> Inside Flex Containers.** Interactive buttons, menu items, and badges already define an internal gap (e.g. <Button> declares `inline-flex items-center gap-2`). Adding `className="mr-2"` or `className="ml-1"` on an <Icon> injects an additional 8px or 4px margin on top of the flex gap, causing an asymmetrical, unbalanced optical gap (16px instead of 8px). Let the parent container's `gap-*` govern spacing naturally.

- **No Sparkle/Magic Icons for AI features** (`stars`, `auto_awesome`). Use functional icons that describe the actual task (e.g., `translate`, `summarize`, `spellcheck`, `tune`, `filter`, `bolt` — match the specific action verb, never default to a generic "brain" or magic icon).
- **No Emojis** in buttons, headers, or list items.
- **No AI Copywriting Clichés & Inflated Nouns:** Avoid generic corporate AI vocabulary: "Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash", "Next-Gen", "Delve", "Tapestry", "Beacon", "Foster", "Testament", "Spearhead", "Harness", "Dynamic", "Robust", "Transformative". Also avoid faux-academic and enterprise inflation words: "Repository", "Matrix", "Enclave", "Infrastructure", "Apparatus", "Telemetric", "Registry". Avoid formulaic 3-bullet lists with bold prefixes for simple explanations when concise direct prose or a table is clearer.
- **No Inflated UI Microcopy (Call Things What They Are):** Use plain, everyday terms for features, sections, and items ("Caseload", "Patients", "Notes", "Exercises", "Saved"). Keep labels to 1–2 simple nouns; never stack 3+ nouns together or append pseudo-technical nouns ("Hub", "Matrix", "System", "Engine", "Repository", "Store", "Registry", "Apparatus").
- **No Developer / Database / CSS Jargon in UI Copy:** Write UI text from the user's perspective ("Patient added", "Saved to patient record", "Preview: Full width"). Never expose backend mechanics, state-machine terms, or CSS layout vocabulary (*"committed"*, *"synchronized"*, *"hydrated"*, *"unconstrained"*, *"instantiated"*, *"dispatched"*, *"affordance"*, *"telemetry"*, *"workflow"*, *"protocol"*, *"executing"*) in UI text.
- **No Fabricated Technical or Compliance Claims:** Never invent architectural, security, encryption, or regulatory claims not specified by the user (e.g., "Firebase Enclave Security", "HIPAA-Certified Vault", "Zero-Knowledge Encryption", "AI Neural Core"). If a badge, chip, or banner needs to display system or data status, use verifiable user/app state only (e.g., "Saved", "Synced", "Offline").
- **No Fabricated Data:** Never invent metrics, uptime percentages, or stats the user hasn't provided. Use `[metric]` placeholders instead. No fake "SYSTEM PERFORMANCE METRICS" / "BY THE NUMBERS" dashboard sections filled with invented numbers.
- **No `LABEL // YEAR` formatting** ("SYSTEM // 2024") — this is a lazy AI convention, not real typography.
- **No Ambiguous Link/Action Text:** Never use "click here" or "click this link" as a link's visible/only accessible text — it describes the mechanic, not the destination, and is meaningless out of context to a screen reader user navigating a links list. Generic phrases `DESIGN.md` already sanctions for buttons (`Learn more`, `Read more`, `View guide`) are fine visually, but if a page has more than one, each needs a unique, descriptive accessible name (e.g. `aria-label="Learn more about breathing exercises"`) rather than relying on identical visible text alone.

## Code Quality & Implementation Integrity

- **No Lazy Placeholder Stubs:** Never emit incomplete placeholders (`// TODO: implement remaining logic`, `// ... rest of code`, `// handle other cases here`, `/* more props */`). All written code must be complete, valid, and runnable. If a branch is explicitly deferred, throw a descriptive guard: `throw new Error('Not implemented: [specific feature]')`.
- **No Type Suppression / `any` Escape Hatches:** Never use `any`, `as any`, `@ts-ignore`, or `as unknown as T` to sidestep type safety. Use proper discriminated unions, generic type constraints, or runtime schema validation/narrowing (`zod`/type guards).

## Overlays & Navigation

- **Banned: Titleless Dialogs, Drawers, Sheets, and Overlays.** Every modal overlay must render an accessible title subcomponent (<DialogTitle>, <DrawerTitle>, <SheetTitle>). Base UI overlay primitives bind their accessibility tree `aria-labelledby` directly to the title. If the visual design does not display a visible title, you must render `<DialogTitle className="sr-only">Title</DialogTitle>` so screen readers announce the dialog context rather than a generic "Dialog".
- **Banned: Manual `z-index` Escalation on Overlays.** Base UI overlays render into top-layer portal roots (<Dialog.Portal>, <Popover.Portal>). Never sprinkle manual `z-50`, `z-9999`, or arbitrary z-index utilities on dialog, sheet, drawer, or popover surfaces. Hardcoded z-indices initiate z-index arms races and break stacking order with toasts and tooltips.

- **Context-appropriate overlays:** Avoid heavy background blurs for simple inputs — use inline editing, side panels, or a properly scaled dialog.
- **Maximize header real estate** before adding a new navigation tier — consolidate search, global actions, and primary nav into one well-organized top bar.

## Component Library & Architecture

- **Banned: Manual `relative`/`absolute` Button or Icon Overlays Inside Inputs.** Never position buttons, icons, or clear triggers over inputs using manual coordinate hacks (`<div className="relative"><Input className="pr-10" /><Button className="absolute right-1">`). This causes focus ring clipping, destroys Windows High Contrast Mode borders, and causes responsive overlap. Always use <InputGroup> + <InputGroupAddon> + <InputGroupButton> / <InputGroupInput> from `@/components/ui/input-group`.
- **Banned: Looped Standalone Buttons for Mode/Filter Selection.** Never loop or map raw <Button> elements (`['Daily', 'Weekly'].map(...)`) to build segmented controls, view pickers, or filter bars. Separate buttons pollute the keyboard Tab order with multiple tab stops and lack grouped ARIA semantics. For 2–7 mutually exclusive or multi-select option sets, mandate <ToggleGroup> (`role="radiogroup"` or `role="group"` with arrow-key roving tabindex).
- **Banned: Ungrouped Menu & Select Items in Popups (*Item outside *Group).** In dropdown menus, context menus, and select popups, items must not float as naked children in the listbox. Wrap items in their semantic group (<Select.Group>, <Menu.Group>, <ContextMenu.Group>) with optional group labels (<Select.GroupLabel>). Screen readers use groups to calculate item counts and navigation boundaries.
- **Banned: Cosmetic `className` Overrides at Component Call Sites.** When consuming primitives, `className` passed to the component root is reserved strictly for layout, positioning, and container bounds (`w-full`, `max-w-md`, `mt-4`, `grid-cols-2`, `shrink-0`). Never override background colors, borders, font families, or radii via `className` at call sites (`<Button className="bg-red-500 rounded-none">`). Colors, surfaces, and variants must be governed exclusively through official design system props (`variant`, `colorRole`, `prominence`, `size`).
- **Banned: Pending Tool Approvals Buried Inside <Trace> Accordions.** In AI agent interactions, never nest pending interactive authorization buttons (`[Approve] / [Deny]`) inside expandable <Trace> or thinking steps. If the trace is collapsed, the user will miss the blocking confirmation request. Pending approvals must render as prominent standalone prompts outside the trace (<ApprovalCard>), collapsing into an audit trace line only after action is taken.
- **Banned: Ad-hoc `@keyframes` & Custom Shimmer Sweeps.** Never author bespoke `@keyframes` or hand-rolled inline gradient sweeps (such as `animate-pulse bg-gradient-to-r from-transparent via-white/20 to-transparent`) at feature or component call sites. Dynamic sweeps and loading states must use the centralized design system primitives:
  - For streaming text, AI reasoning, and active transcription highlights: use `<ShimmerText>` from `@/components/ui/shimmer-text` (built on `@utility animate-shimmer-sweep`).
  - For spatial loading placeholders: use `<Skeleton>` or `<SkeletonReveal>` from `@/components/ui/skeleton` (`animate-pulse rounded bg-surface-variant`).
  Both automatically enforce `motion-reduce:animate-none` for WCAG 2.2 AA compliance and maintain centralized brand timing tokens.
- **Banned: Stripping Semantic Triggers via Polymorphic Downgrades (The `render={<span />}` Trigger Trap).** Never use Base UI's `render` prop on interactive disclosure triggers (`Collapsible.Trigger`, `TraceCollapsibleTrigger`, `Accordion.Trigger`, `Dialog.Trigger`) to downgrade the trigger to a plain `<span>`, `<div>`, or non-interactive container. Replacing the native button element destroys keyboard focus (`tabIndex={0}`), Enter/Space keydown handlers, and accessibility tree roles (`button`, `aria-expanded`). Triggers must always render as true semantic buttons that wrap content ("Behavior Wraps the Line").

- **No Radix imports in new code** — this project's primitive foundation is Base UI. See component-library.md.
- **No hand-rolled components that duplicate an existing primitive.** See the primitive-check-first workflow in the core contract and component-library.md.
- **Banned: Raw HTML Interactive Controls Outside Primitives.** Never write `<button>`, `<input>`, `<select>`, `<textarea>`, or native `<dialog>` directly in composite blocks, features, or pages. Direct interactive tags bypass Base UI focus management, floating focus rings, and design tokens. Always import `<Button>`, `<Input>`, `<Select>`, `<Textarea>`, `<Dialog>` from `@/components/ui/` (enforced via ESLint AST selectors). When an unstyled semantic button is required (e.g. card stretched-link triggers, sortable table headers, or custom row triggers that must not inherit 48px CTA button chrome), import and use `<UnstyledButton>` or `<Button variant="unstyled">` from `@/components/ui/button`.
- **Banned: Registry Bloat (Domain Leaks into Shared Registry).** Never place app-specific business logic or domain views (e.g., speech therapy presentation views, articulation drill games) into `src/components/blocks/` or the shared component registry. The shared registry contains only domain-agnostic, reusable UI compositions. Domain views belong in `src/features/` or `src/views/`.
- **Banned: Toxic Wrapper-itis (`<Box>`, `<Flex>`, `<Spacer>`) vs Pragmatic Layout `<div>`s.** Do not create artificial wrapper components to eliminate standard layout `<div>`s. Using plain `<div className="flex flex-col gap-4">` or `<div className="grid grid-cols-12">` for layout is standard, efficient, and encouraged. Component abstraction is reserved for interactive controls and semantic structures.
- **Banned: Stateful Flows Disguised as Generic UI Blocks.** Do not package login screens, OAuth handlers, or streaming chatbot flows with hardcoded auth or network layers into generic UI block directories (`components/blocks/`). Stateful flows coupled to auth tokens or streaming protocols belong in `src/features/` as application code or starter recipes.

## Skeuomorphism & Shadows

- **Banned: Single-Layer Muddy Drop Shadows.** A single-stop harsh drop shadow (e.g. `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0,0,0,0.2)` or `shadow-lg` defaults). Creates an unrefined, muddy halo. Always use the multi-stop layered tokens (`shadow-ambient` or `shadow-modal`) where a tight contact shadow is paired with a softer outer shadow.
- **Banned: Pure Black Shadows.** Never use pure pitch-black `rgba(0,0,0,...)` for ambient UI elevation. Shadows must use the calibrated ink tint `rgba(26, 28, 30, ...)`.
- **Banned: Faux Shadow Borders (Shadow Rings).** Never substitute a real CSS `border` with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px ...` (`smooth-shadow-ring`). In Windows High Contrast Mode (`forced-colors: active`), `box-shadow` is stripped entirely by the browser, rendering the container borderless and invisible. Overlays must maintain real physical borders (`border-outline-variant`).
- **Banned: Floating Everything.** Buttons, nav bars, search inputs, chips, and inline alerts must NOT have box shadows. Elevation is strictly budgeted to Standard Content Cards and Tier A/B floating overlays (Exception: Sticky Navigation Bar has zero shadow at rest, but applies `shadow-ambient` dynamically via `scroll-state(stuck: top)` when scrolled over content).
- **Banned: Multi-Tier Shadow Menu Slop.** Do not sprinkle arbitrary Tailwind shadow tiers (`shadow-xs` to `shadow-2xl`). All elevation must use the three semantic tokens: `shadow-ambient`, `shadow-ambient-hover`, or `shadow-modal`.
- No button outer glows, neon gradient overlays, or heavy background blurs. Elevation comes from tonal layers and low-contrast outlines; interaction depth uses crisp, ink-tinted multi-stop ambient elevation.

## Motion & Animation (Banned)

- **Banned: Blanket `transition-all` on Containers.** Never use `transition-all` on cards, layout boxes, or dialog wrappers. It forces the browser style engine to recompute layout geometry for all properties on every state change, producing frame drops, stutter, and layout thrashing. Always declare explicit targeted properties: `transition-[box-shadow,border-color,background-color,transform]`, `transition-[opacity,transform]` (for overlays), `transition-[opacity,transform,filter]` (strictly for micro-primitives), or for expandables/accordions, `overflow-hidden h-[var(--accordion-panel-height)] transition-[height]`.
- **Banned: `filter: blur()` on Large Text-Bearing Overlays.** Animating CSS `filter: blur()` across dialogs, drawers, popovers, or menus causes GPU repaint lag, Safari text-rendering flicker, and eye strain for low-vision and dyslexic readers. Optical blur tokens (`--blur-small`, `--blur-medium`) are strictly restricted to in-place micro-primitives (`<TextSwap />`, `<Skeleton.Reveal />`, icon state morphs). Overlays must rely exclusively on crisp `opacity` + `transform` transitions.
- **Banned: Bounce & Spring Easing on Standard UI Chrome.** Playful keyframe springs, overshoot bounces, and rubber-banding are strictly forbidden on overlay containers, dialogs, drawers, switches, and cards. Spring/bounce easing is reserved strictly for solitary celebratory achievements (`SuccessCheck`, celebration confetti). Standard UI chrome strictly uses `--ease-standard` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`).
- **Banned: Uncapped Multi-Item Staggers.** Never calculate dynamic stagger delays using uncapped loops (e.g. `index * 50ms`) across arbitrary list lengths. Delaying items beyond index 5 introduces perceived UI lag and frustrating latency. Always enforce the **5-item cap** ($\text{max delay} \le 200\text{ms}$ at 40ms stagger).
- **Banned: Missing Reduced-Motion Fallbacks.** Shipping transitions without `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` or `motion-reduce:transition-none` violates WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.3 and can cause discomfort for motion-sensitive users. Always strip translation, scale, and blur, preserving instant or opacity-only state changes.
- **Banned: Sub-44px Touch Targets on Dismiss Buttons.** Dismiss triggers on toasts, banners, or notification badges must maintain a minimum 44×44px hit-target (`w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px]`) to comply with WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8. Never squeeze close buttons down to 24px or 28px without an expanded hit target.
- **Banned: Double-Bounce Keyframes on High-Frequency Controls.** Frequent form controls (checkboxes, switches, radio buttons) must not employ 350ms multi-bounce keyframes or drawn-out stroke-draw animations. High-frequency controls use instant or calibrated 150ms micro-transitions to respect user momentum.
